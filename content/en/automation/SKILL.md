# Automation

Doing the same thing over and over? Let's make a computer do it. Where strict rules are needed, I build a tool without AI that occasionally makes things up. And where AI makes sense, I'll help you set it up in plain human terms, without a stiff corporate training where you learn nothing.

Excel is a great tool. Just not for spending your Sundays in. I am a backend engineer from a bank, and after hours I help sole traders and small businesses who are drowning in admin. I have already pulled a few of them out. You could be next.

## When I can help

- You keep doing the same steps over and over, just with different data
- You retype, check or keep an eye on things a computer would handle better, and without complaining
- Every month you put together the same overview, invoice or report by hand
- You want practical, human help with AI on your actual work, not a stiff paid training where you learn nothing
- Not sure whether it calls for AI or automation? Ask. I don't bite.

## Who it's for

- Sole traders: that's who I have helped most so far
- Households: budgets, documents, insurance
- Small businesses with a few people

## How I work

- First I understand what you do by hand and why. Then I suggest the simplest solution. Simplest, not most expensive.
- It runs by fixed rules, not by an AI's mood: same data, same result, every time. If it works without AI, it gets done without AI. AI only where it really helps.
- Sometimes no coding is needed at all: we just sit down with your actual work, set up AI to fit it and skip the fluff.
- You end up with a simple tool or workflow you can run yourself. No 100-page manual.

## Examples

**Input:** A friend imports goods and retyped every shipment into the customs system by hand. His options: quit, or pay a vendor ~CZK 30k a month.
**Output:** An app that turns his Excel sheet into a finished customs declaration and checks it on the way. He now does it from home in a few minutes. Option three won.

**Input:** Family finances in a spreadsheet; on the first of every month, shuffling money between accounts by hand.
**Output:** An app with a monthly plan, a calendar of payments and a month-end close from account balances. The spreadsheet retired.

**Input:** Insurance policies, contracts and their renewal dates scattered across emails and drawers.
**Output:** A home assistant that keeps them in one place and reminds us in time what is about to expire. No more "wait, did that lapse?".

## Not a fit

- Companies with hundreds of people. There you need a project manager, and that project manager needs another project manager.
- 24/7 support with guaranteed availability. I sleep sometimes.
