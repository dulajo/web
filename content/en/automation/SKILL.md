# Automation

I am a backend engineer from banking, and outside work I help people who are drowning in paperwork. I have already done this for a few sole traders. You could be next.

## When to use

- You retype data from Excel into another system or form
- Every month you put together the same overview or month-end close by hand
- Paperwork is pushing you towards an expensive vendor, or towards giving up altogether
- You are not sure whether it can be automated at all. Just ask.

## Who it's for

- Sole traders: that's who I have helped most so far
- Households: budgets, documents, insurance
- Small businesses with a few people

## How I work

- First I understand what you do by hand and why. Then I suggest the simplest thing that does the job.
- If it can be solved without AI, I solve it without AI: it's cheaper and it doesn't make mistakes. I add AI only where it really helps, and always on top of tools that give the same result every time.
- You end up with a simple tool you can run yourself.

## Examples

**Input:** A friend imports goods and retyped every shipment into the customs system by hand. His options: quit, or pay a vendor ~CZK 30k a month.
**Output:** An app that turns his Excel sheet into a finished customs declaration and validates it. He now handles it from home in a few minutes.

**Input:** Family finances in a spreadsheet; on the first of every month, splitting income across accounts by hand.
**Output:** An app with a monthly plan, a calendar of regular payments and a month-end close from account balances.

**Input:** Insurance policies, contracts and their renewal dates scattered across emails and drawers.
**Output:** A home assistant that keeps them in one knowledge base and reminds us in time what is about to expire.

## Not a fit

- Companies with hundreds of people, large enterprise systems and ERP rollouts
- 24/7 support with guaranteed availability
