# Smart home

We built our house and run it on Loxone and Home Assistant. I have it tuned the way I like it, and I am happy to look at yours, however smart it is, or isn't.

## When to use

- You have a smart home, but your devices talk to each other like neighbours over a fence
- You are building or renovating and want to think it through before the plaster goes on
- You don't have a smart home, but you want your paperwork in order: appliances, warranties, insurance, servicing
- You want your own home server (homelab) and your data at home, not "somewhere in the cloud"
- You want a home AI assistant that doesn't make things up

## Principles

- Lights follow rules, not an AI with moods. Nobody wants hallucinating blinds.
- The assistant uses tools and a knowledge base built for it. It doesn't guess, it knows.

## What runs at our place

- Loxone as a solid base, Home Assistant for everything else
- An agent with a knowledge base that holds it all together. It knows when we bought each appliance and when its warranty ends, which insurance policies we have and what they cost, when each service is due and when it was last done. It can't change the house's settings and writes to the knowledge base only with our approval. It's an agent, not the boss.
- A homelab with our own services and monitoring
- A 3D model of the whole house, because why not

## Not a fit

- Electrical installation. I'm a programmer, not an electrician, and I'd like to keep my eyebrows.
- 24/7 on-call support. If the living room refuses to turn purple at midnight, it can wait until morning.
