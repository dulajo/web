# Smart home

We built our house and run it through smart controls, and I enjoy it more every year. I have it tuned the way I like it, and I am happy to help you with yours.

## When to use

- You are building or renovating and want to think the smart home through up front
- You have Loxone or Home Assistant and the automations don't do what you want
- You want a home server (homelab) for your own services and your data at home
- You want a home AI assistant that doesn't make things up

## What runs at our place

- A house run through Loxone and Home Assistant
- A homelab with our own services and monitoring
- A home assistant we chat with. It sees the house and our knowledge base and keeps track of every insurance policy, its renewal and its settings. It can't change the house's settings, and it writes to the knowledge base only after we approve.

## Principles

- If it can be deterministic, it should be. Lights, heating and blinds follow rules, not a model that reads the situation differently every time.
- The assistant uses tools and a knowledge base that were built for it. It doesn't guess.

## Not a fit

- Electrical installation and wiring: that's a job for an electrician
- 24/7 support with guaranteed response times
