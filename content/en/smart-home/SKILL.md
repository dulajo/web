# Smart home

We built our house and run it through smart controls, and I enjoy it more every year. I have it tuned the way I like it, and I am happy to help with yours, however smart it is, or isn't.

## When to use

- You have a smart home and want it to work better, or want your devices to finally talk to each other
- You are building or renovating and want to think the smart home through up front
- You don't have a smart home, but you want your paperwork in order: appliances, warranties, insurance, servicing
- You want a home server (homelab) for your own services and your data at home
- You want a home AI assistant that doesn't make things up

## Principles

- If it can be deterministic, it should be. Lights, heating and blinds follow rules, not a model that reads the situation differently every time.
- The assistant uses tools and a knowledge base that were built for it. It doesn't guess.

## What runs at our place

- Loxone as a solid base, Home Assistant for everything else
- An agent with a knowledge base that ties it all together. It knows when we bought each appliance and when its warranty ends, which insurance policies we have and what they cost, when each service is due and when it was last done. It can't change the house's settings, and it writes to the knowledge base only after we approve.
- A homelab with our own services and monitoring
- A 3D model of the whole house

## Not a fit

- Electrical installation and wiring: that's a job for an electrician
- 24/7 support with guaranteed response times
